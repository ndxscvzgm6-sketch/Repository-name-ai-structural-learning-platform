# THE 12-MONTH SPRINT — Rev 5 (fast-runner calibration)
Goal: a $200k+ TC offer in hand by month 12. Assumes 15–20 focused hrs/week
outside work. Nothing below waits on tenure; only third-party posting minimums
are treated as hard, and we route around them.

## Month 0–1 · LAUNCH (everything starts simultaneously)
- Day 1–3: LinkedIn rewrite live (Headline A), OTW recruiters-only, resume final.
- Week 1: APPLY — you clear these bars TODAY: AECOM remote DC structural
  ($150–200k, needs 5–6 yrs buildings only), Jacobs Sr DC structural, kW/WSP,
  Burns & McDonnell, HDR, Stantec, Vantage; PLUS Exponent Senior Engineer
  (facade-failure niche — their bar is MS+PE+depth, not years); PLUS Google
  civil/structural 3-yr band and AWS FSE-CSA as lottery tickets. 15+ apps,
  every one with a referral touch.
- Week 2–4: Anchorage portfolio: all 3 worked calcs done at 7-22 factors
  (3 weekends, not 12 weeks). Article #1 drafted and SUBMITTED to STRUCTURE.
  SE exam: register the next available section. NIW attorney engaged.
- Aug: AECtech Boston hackathon (it's next month — go).

## Month 2–4 · CONVERT WAVE 1
- Target outcome: Step-1 offer at $150–200k (DC design firm or Exponent).
  Take the best; KEEP interviewing upward — loyalty is for year 3.
- Ship bridge project #1 (structural Python tool) M2; #2 (plan-review or
  vibration tool) M4. NIW filed. Tilt-up capstone calc done (2 weekends).
- Content: 2 posts/wk from day 1; 800–1,500 followers by M4.

## Month 5–8 · STACK EVIDENCE INSIDE THE SEAT
- In the new seat: claim mission-critical/investigation scope aggressively;
  volunteer for every Cx witness, vendor audit, site rotation. Every project
  is a resume line within 90 days, not a year.
- SE section #2. Article #2 (ASCE Forensic Congress or JPCF). Register as
  journal/conference reviewer the week article #1 publishes.
- Master-curriculum items 1, 2, 5 (7-22 deltas, DG11+VC vibration, forensic
  method) — ~45h total ≈ 6 weeks at your pace.

## Month 9–12 · ATTACK THE $200k TIER
- With 6–9 months of named DC/forensic projects + PE + (near-)SE + 2 articles
  + shipped tools: apply Google 5-yr band (argue 7 yrs total + portfolio),
  Microsoft senior CSA, Oracle, Vantage senior, Exponent Sr Managing track,
  Palantir FDSE. Batch applications so offers collide; negotiate on the
  collision.
- Month 12 target: ≥1 offer ≥$200k TC. Fallback that still wins: you're at
  $150–200k with a compounding portfolio — the same endpoint, one wave later.

## What stays multi-year (honesty, not slowness)
EB-1A evidence density (~2029 filing), first-chair testimony, SE license
completion (exam calendar is fixed). Everything else above is 12 months.
