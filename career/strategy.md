## The H-1B/green-card thread (runs under everything)

1. **Transfers are your friend**: in-country H-1B change-of-employer petitions are exempt from the $100k fee; no lottery. Put "H-1B — transfer eligible, no lottery, not subject to the $100k new-petition fee" in recruiter conversations (never on the public profile).
2. **Never put visa status on LinkedIn or the resume** — recruiters boolean-NOT phrases like "H1B"/"sponsorship". There is no visa filter in LinkedIn Recruiter; don't hand them one. Answer application knockout questions truthfully.
3. **File an EB-2 NIW self-petition ASAP** (structural engineers working on infrastructure safety + AI tooling have documented approvals) — it frees you from employer-tethered PERM and locks a priority date. If India-born, the EB-2 date backlog is 15–25 years, so simultaneously **build the EB-1A/O-1 evidence base**: publications (STRUCTURE magazine, ASCE journals), open-source adoption, hackathon wins, code-committee membership, talks, judging. Every Track B artifact is also immigration evidence. This is the real exit ramp.
4. **Prioritize proven sponsors**: Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle, NVIDIA, Autodesk (255 LCAs/yr), Procore, Palantir, OpenAI, Exponent. Startups sponsor case-by-case — negotiable but verify before investing.
