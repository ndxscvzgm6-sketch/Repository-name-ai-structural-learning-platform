# Advanced Curriculum — Rev 4 (calibrated to PE-Structural + MS baseline)

**Correction:** earlier structural courses partly re-taught licensure-level
material. This supersedes the Phase-0 study plan. Rule: skip anything you were
examined on; study only the expert deltas below.

## What to SKIP in the existing courses (you know it)
- Tilt-Up L1–L3, most of L6–L7 · Anchorage L2–L4 · Precast L2–L3
- KEEP (genuinely non-baseline): Tilt-Up L4–L5 + capstone (slender-wall §11.8
  is rarely practiced in high-rise work), L8–L10 (Northridge chain,
  lifting/bracing = construction engineering), Anchorage L5–L6 + 3 calcs
  (option strategy, isolator 2× rule, pad dowels, review craft), PEMB L5–L7
  (delegated-design gotchas), and ALL of MCFE + DC Literacy (cross-domain).

## The MASTER-LEVEL curriculum (what a PE+MS should actually study)
1. **Code deltas, not code basics** — ASCE 7-22 vs 7-16 in anger: the full new
   Ch.13 (Hf/Rμ/CAR/Rpo tables), new tornado Ch.32, diaphragm design changes
   (12.10.3 alternative), and ACI 318-25 changes. Source: ASCE 7-22 significant
   changes guide + SEAOC blogs. (~15h)
2. **Floor vibration at expert level** — AISC DG11 2nd ed. full method,
   sensitive-equipment generic VC velocity curves, footfall FE analysis,
   measurement/baselining. This is the DC differentiator nobody has. (~15h)
3. **Performance-based & nonlinear** — FEMA P-58 loss assessment, ASCE 41
   existing-building nonlinear procedures (your forensic edge), pushover/NLRHA
   literacy. Owners increasingly buy "functional recovery." (~25h)
4. **Progressive collapse / disproportionate collapse** — UFC 4-023-03 tie
   forces & alternate path; mission-critical owners cite it. (~10h)
5. **Forensic method at expert level** — ASCE Guidelines for Failure
   Investigation, standard-of-care doctrine, Daubert/expert-report craft,
   fractography & concrete petrography literacy. (~15h)
6. **Delegated design & structural risk law** — responsibility case law,
   AIA/EJCDC risk allocation, vendor-calc audit method. The owner's-engineer
   judgment layer. (~10h)
7. **Computational depth (Track B)** — OpenSeesPy nonlinear models, ML
   surrogates with honest validation, building the plan-review prototype. (~25h)

Deliverables stay unchanged (they were never basic): tilt-up capstone calc,
3 anchorage calcs at 7-22 factors, published article, review checklist.
